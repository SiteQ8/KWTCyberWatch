How to read this register

Each entry is what a public tracker, a press report or a forum post claims. Criminals inflate, recycle and sometimes invent their listings, so an entry is a reason to check, not a fact. If your organisation appears here, verify through your own incident response process and notify the authorities and your regulator as required.

The register collects only the organisation named, the date, the actor and a link to the public page. No leaked data is hosted, copied or linked. It updates itself several times a day. If an entry is wrong, fake or resolved, use the report link on the entry or write to site@hotmail.com. The raw list is available as JSON.
