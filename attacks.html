How to read this list

A leak-site listing or a forum post is a claim made by a criminal, and claims are sometimes inflated, recycled or false. Treat each entry as a reason to check, not as a fact. If your organisation appears here, verify through your own incident response process and notify the authorities and your regulator as required. Entries carry only what the public source states: the organisation named, the date, the actor and a link. No leaked data is hosted, copied or linked here.

The list updates itself several times a day from the public sources. If an entry is wrong, fake, resolved or about your organisation and you want it reviewed, use the report link on the row or write to site@hotmail.com.
